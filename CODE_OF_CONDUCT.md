# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about code, not about the person
writing it.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason attached.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way.
- Respect that maintainers and contributors have limited time and different
  priorities than yours.
- Keep discussion in public channels where it belongs, so decisions stay
  reviewable.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery — including anything targeting age, body
  size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality,
  personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.
- Using this project's issue tracker or pull requests to attack a third party.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit comments — and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately by opening a
[security advisory](https://github.com/KazKozDev/WebOperator/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/KazKozDev). Reports are handled confidentially;
the reporter's identity is not shared with the person reported.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what happens, proportionate to what occurred: a private
warning, an edit or removal of the offending content, a temporary block, or a
permanent ban from the project. Enforcement decisions are final.
